Welcome to our General Medicine

Provides expert care for a wide range of illnesses like fever, infections, hypertension, and diabetes. Our internal medicine team ensures accurate diagnosis, medical management, and preventive health education for all age groups.

Welcome to our Gynecology

From adolescence to menopause, we offer complete women’s healthcare—pregnancy care, menstrual disorders, fertility consultation, and surgeries. Our gynecology wing now includes dedicated services and specialists for holistic support.

Welcome to our Orthopedics

Manages bone, joint, and muscle-related issues with modern techniques. From fracture care to joint replacements, our orthopedic department focuses on quick recovery, pain management, and rehabilitation through physiotherapy.

Welcome to our Urology

Diagnosis and treatment for urinary and male reproductive issues such as kidney stones, prostate enlargement, and infections. Our urologists combine medical and surgical approaches for long-term solutions.

Welcome to our Pediatrics

Child-friendly care for newborns to teenagers. We handle vaccinations, infections, growth monitoring, and emergency care. Our NICU setup supports critical neonatal care with round-the-clock pediatricians.

Welcome to our Cardiology

Advanced heart care for conditions like chest pain, high BP, and heart disease. Our team performs diagnostics like ECG and ECHO, and provides emergency intervention and lifestyle guidance.

Welcome to our Nutrition

We offer personalized diet counseling for managing lifestyle diseases, weight loss, pregnancy nutrition, and recovery diets. Our nutritionists help you make informed food choices for a healthy life.

Welcome to our Physiotherapy

Post-surgery recovery, sports injury rehab, and pain management are handled through guided therapies and exercises. Our expert physiotherapists create customized plans for mobility and strength.
